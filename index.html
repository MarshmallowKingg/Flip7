Scoring rules
  • Add up your number cards (0–12).
  • ×2 doubles your number cards only, applied before modifiers.
  • Add +2 to +10 modifier cards.
  • Flip 7 (7 different number cards) = +15 bonus and ends the round.
  • Bust (duplicate number) = 0 for the round. Second Chance / Freeze / Flip Three score nothing.
